Orbit

A personal data hub: trading, budget, planning, market news & a live bot

A full local command-center built from scratch: a FastAPI launcher serving several HTML/CSS/JS dashboards inside one hub. Each app turns raw data into something readable — trades, money, tasks, markets — with charts, KPIs and projections everywhere. A Python bot wired to the Hyperliquid exchange places real orders (entry, stop-loss, take-profit), piloted from Telegram.

📈 Trading journal & analytics 💰 Budget, assets & wealth projection ✅ To-do & daily planning 🎯 Polymarket capital tracker 📰 Crypto-news watch 🤖 Hyperliquid bot (Telegram)

Polybot-Tracker

End-to-end research on prediction-market strategies

A full data pipeline and backtesting suite to study strategy profitability on Polymarket. I reverse-engineered a professional bot (~$12M profit) from ~36,000 trades, then stress-tested every hypothesis on real historical fills — not theoretical quotes.

Polycheat

On-chain copy-trading bot on a 24/7 cloud server

A bot that mirrors high-performing traders detected on-chain in real time (~1.3s) over WebSocket. Clean architecture separates engine, simulation and real execution — safe by design, dry-run by default, deployed and running on a free-forever Linux VM.

  • Real-time on-chain detection of many wallets at once via QuickNode RPC WebSocket.
  • Wallet scanner with SQLite exclusion memory — never re-analyses a rejected wallet.
  • Per-order / per-session / daily caps, file kill-switch, private key only from env vars.
  • Deployed on Oracle Cloud (won capacity after 27 automated retries), systemd Restart=always.

⚖️ Signal vs. artefact

Arbitrage & Fair-Value Lab

Latency-aware arbitrage & Black-Scholes option pricing

Research tools on two strategies: pair arbitrage and crypto-option fair value. A paper-execution probe measures what would really get filled given latency, and a Black-Scholes model prices Polymarket "BTC Up or Down" against live Binance spot.

  • 2-leg micro-executor with leg-risk handling (auto-flatten if only one leg fills).
  • Black-Scholes edge collector comparing implied vs realised volatility over ~44h of live data.
  • Key finding: the apparent edge is a stale-quote / volatility artefact — not exploitable alpha.

About

How I work

🎓 Data science graduate — double degree from Mines Saint-Étienne & Institut Polytechnique de Paris. Now looking for a data-related role.

I turn data into tools people can actually use — dashboards, analyses and automations — and I build them end-to-end, from idea to production, testing my own assumptions with real numbers even when that means proving myself wrong.

Most of my recent work sits at the intersection of data analysis and software engineering: collecting data at scale over REST and WebSocket, turning it into dashboards and visualisations, backtesting strategies over millions of records, validating out-of-sample, and shipping the result as a running service on a Linux cloud server.

What I care about most is telling real signal from artefact — a survivorship bias, a stale quote, an implied-vs-realised volatility gap — and being honest about it. Across one research project I invalidated five strategies with evidence and kept only the one that survived rigorous validation.

I'm methodical when things break: isolate causes by elimination rather than guessing, keep guardrails and dry-run modes around anything that touches real money, and document as I go.

I also work fast by pairing with AI — Claude Code is my everyday pair-programmer. It handles the boilerplate, but the architecture, the trade-offs and the debugging are mine; I make a point of understanding every line I ship.

What sets my work apart

  • Scientific rigor — hypotheses tested empirically, ideas invalidated with numbers.
  • Full autonomy — from idea to production deployment, solo.
  • Methodical debugging — cause isolation by elimination.
  • Risk-first mindset — systematic guardrails, simulate before going live.
  • Critical thinking — separating true signal from artefact.
